01 Browser extension

Apex Log Insights

A free, offline toolkit for Apex debug logs. Drop in a .log file and get five structured views: a triage verdict, the 20-phase execution timeline, SOQL and DML against governor limits, diagnostics, and a searchable raw log. Every finding links back to its exact line.

03 Salesforce app

Configurable CSV uploads inside Lightning. An admin defines an upload process once — object, operation, allowed columns, matching rules — and users work from its template. Apex enforces the user’s own permissions, processes rows in the background, and reports the outcome of every row.
